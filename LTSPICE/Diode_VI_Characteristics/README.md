# Diode V-I Characteristics

## Objective
To simulate and analyze the Voltage-Current (V-I) characteristics of a 1N4148 diode using LTspice.

## Circuit
- Diode: 1N4148
- Resistor: 1 kΩ
- DC voltage sweep: 0 V to 5 V
- Sweep step: 0.01 V

## Simulation
The diode was forward biased and its voltage-current characteristic was obtained using a DC sweep.

## Result
The simulated V-I characteristic shows that the diode current remains very small at low forward voltages and increases rapidly after the forward conduction region.

## Tool
LTspice
